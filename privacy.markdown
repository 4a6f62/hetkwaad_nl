# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 15, 2026*

## 1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to hetkwaad.nl. For questions, you can reach out via [GitHub](https://github.com/4a6f62){:target="_blank"} or [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@4a6f62){:target="_blank"}.

## 2. Hosting

This site is a static Jekyll site hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). GitHub processes standard technical logs (such as IP address) as part of serving the page. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement){:target="_blank"}.

## 3. No custom fonts, no analytics

This site does not load any third-party fonts and does not use analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts of its own.

## 4. Videos (YouTube)

Video posts show a "Click to load video" button. **Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click that button yourself.** Once you click, a privacy-enhanced YouTube embed (`youtube-nocookie.com`) is loaded; YouTube/Google may then set cookies and process data, particularly if you play the video. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy){:target="_blank"}.

## 5. External links

Links to YouTube and GitHub lead to third-party services with their own privacy policies. This policy only covers hetkwaad.nl itself.

## 6. Your rights

For the processing described in section 4, you can contact Google to exercise your rights (such as access or deletion). Questions about this site itself can be directed to the contact channels in section 1. You can also file a complaint with the [Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens)](https://www.autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl){:target="_blank"}.
