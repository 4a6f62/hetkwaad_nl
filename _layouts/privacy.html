Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

1. Who is responsible

This policy applies to 4a6f62.com. For questions, you can reach out via LinkedIn or GitHub.

2. Hosting

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). GitHub processes standard technical logs (such as IP address) as part of serving the page. See GitHub's privacy statement.

3. Fonts (Google Fonts)

To display text, your browser fetches fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). This transmits your IP address to Google. This happens on every visit, even without clicking anything. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Videos (YouTube)

The page has a button to view videos. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click that button yourself. Once you click, a privacy-enhanced YouTube embed (youtube-nocookie.com) is loaded; YouTube/Google may then set cookies and process data, particularly if you play a video. See Google's privacy policy.

5. No own tracking

This site does not use its own analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking scripts. No contact form or other form is offered that would collect personal data.

6. External links

Links to YouTube, GitHub, and LinkedIn lead to third-party services with their own privacy policies. This policy only covers 4a6f62.com itself.

7. Your rights

For the processing described in sections 3 and 4, you can contact Google to exercise your rights (such as access or deletion). Questions about this site itself can be directed to the contact channels in section 1. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
